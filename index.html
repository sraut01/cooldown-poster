Same Loss, Same Noise, Opposite Schedules

Subha Raut, Subham Singh, Ashutosh Mishra · Mississippi State University

SGD takes steps that follow the slope, so they shrink on their own and it settles at the bottom. signSGD (an Adam-like method) takes fixed-length steps and keeps hopping around the bottom. With a cooldown, its steps shrink at the end of training and it settles too.
